# SNAM v1: hypotheses, estimands, and decision rules

**Freeze before discovery.** This is an exploratory mechanistic study. No interventions will be described as a fixed 'personality'. All model weights remain frozen.

## I. Mathematical scope

Given language definition tau and frozen model theta, construct a next-token lexical readout:

- P_tau^+, P_tau^- over tokenizer IDs (both fixed prior to neuron selection).
- J_tau(x;u) = E_{t~P+} logit_t(x,u) - E_{t~P-} logit_t(x,u).
- M_tau(x;u) = mean-logP(y+|x,u) - mean-logP(y-|x,u), where y+ and y- are independently authored continuations.

`J` and `M` are **different** outcomes. A stronger J does not automatically mean visible target style. The forced margin `M` is the shared *transfer* outcome on untouched heldout prompts. Independent free generation must be separately judged.

### Compiler families

- **L lexical / strict**: semantic phrase vs user-supplied neutral phrase -> token frequencies.
- **D distribution / strict**: input-token embedding centroid -> cosine ranked tokenizer entries -> softmax on top 256 only. Pre-trained parameters only; explicit temperature.
- **C counterfactual / data-assisted**: positive/negative continuations of discovery-only prompts -> signed unigram frequency after overlap cancellation. No fitting/updating model weights; **does consume authored examples**.
- **S self-generated expansion / optional**: text generated by the frozen model before freeze may be substituted into the semantic descriptions. This is not strict zero-data and is not included in primary 3×2 design.
- **Logical concept composition / follow-up**: define concept constraints and predict conjunction/negation effects. Not tested by these data; must be preregistered as separate study.
- **SAE feature basis / follow-up**: requires external pretrained SAE weights (separate identity & representation error checks). Not a pure-original-weights method and not part of primary factorial test.

### Solver families

- **W weight-only**: `g^W_ln = W_down[:,n]^T U^T(q+ - q-) / ||W_down[:,n]||`. No contextual propagation. It is a **direct-output projection proxy**, not the derivative of J at intermediate layers.
- **G Jacobian**: `g^G_ln = E_discovery[ d J_tau(x;u) / du_ln at u=0 ]`, with activation injection `alpha=u/||W_down[:,n]||`, computed by autograd. Context-dependent, not weight-only closed form.

Both rank single-neuron absolute slopes and return +/− signs. **No runtime p50 calibration enters ranking or dosing**: each unit of dose produces a single-layer direct residual-output perturbation with unit L2 norm; this maintains the strict weight-only condition. For support S of size 1 or 2 with total standardized L2 dose `b`:
`u_S = b*g_S/||g_S||`; for desired linearized change delta:
`u_S* = delta*g_S/(g_S^T g_S)` (only if `||u*||<=b`). The result is **exact for the stated linear-quadratic surrogate and fixed S**, not for free-form generation.

## II. Testable hypotheses

| ID | Claim | Statistical/computational test | Falsification/limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| H0 | No-op injection preserves baseline | zero-hook vs baseline on fixed prompts | any discrepancy must be debugged before causal claims |
| H1 | W from raw weights finds lexical-expression controls | W Top-K vs layer-/norm-matched and own-sign random; paired heldout M | null or detrimental performance in new contexts |
| H2 | G improves over W | same traits/prompts, prompt-paired method comparison; same dose & candidate budget | no reliable improvement, especially after extra compute |
| H3 | G predicts **local** response magnitude | same-input central finite difference vs autograd in `calibrate.py` | slope sign mismatch / large relative error even at small epsilon |
| H4 | ranking transfers discovery -> heldout | discovery LOO Top-64 retention; heldout J & M, cross-language/domain; teacher-forcing and blind freegen | strong J but weak M/free generation -> proxy mismatch |
| H5 | semantic compiler matters | L/D/C compared under same W/G solver; contrast data resource explicitly reported | no reproducible difference; C is not an equal-data comparison |
| H6 | sign specificity | Top signs vs exact same coordinates reversed | no asymmetric or reversal behavior |
| H7 | sparse analytic combinations add benefit | 2-coordinate controls at same L2 budget vs 1-coordinate | no gain; cannot infer nonlinear synergy from linear surrogate |
| H8 | trait-specific rather than generic disruption | optionally test controls against *other* trait margins | every trait increases identically or fluency degrades |
| H9 | no damage to task expression | blind human fluency/relevance/degeneration alongside trait intensity | linguistic corruption counted as failure, not success |
| H10 | minimal observability | word-only lexical shift J vs full continuation M vs blind free generation | divergence suggests text-frequency bias |

H1/H2/H4/H5/H6/H7/H8 are *exploratory effect hypotheses*, not yet population-universal statements. No multiple-testing correction in v1; raw p values only diagnostic.

## III. Prespecified design and budgets

1. 3 expressive traits (hostility, enthusiasm, pessimism), not decision-level personality. Positive/neutral definition in `semantics.json`.
2. 6 discovery and 12 heldout contrast prompts per trait. English and Chinese (not full balance); phrase templates share structure so prompts are not iid draws from the real world.
3. Primary factorial: 3 compilers × 2 solvers × 3 traits. Same candidate budget Top-4, random-4, per-neuron standardized `dose=0.5`, 6 heldout prompts for pilot. Full study: all 12 heldout, Top-10, random-10 (new validation output/run settings). **Pilot is not the confirmation study.**
4. Freeze full 54 contrast rows plus semantic spec before looking at any rank. Heldout never used in discovery, compiler C limited to discovery rows. Do not edit heldout or trait definitions after results. In new runs, revised hypotheses require a new audit manifest.
5. Analytic pairs evaluated at same total standardized L2 dose, and distinguished from individual doses. Do not claim pairs beat single support based only on linear proxy.
6. Positive outcome: higher heldout forced margin M without worse blinded fluency/relevance; prespecify a practical threshold on human ratings before a separate confirmatory run. **No single p<0.05 gate for this pilot.**
7. Analyze prompt-level paired averages, not coordinates/tokens/orders as independent units. Bootstrap over prompts (illustrative only for templated dependencies); match Top coordinate ranks to random controls with their own gradient/proxy sign.
8. Record `weight-only` scores as **proxy units**, never put their magnitude into a shared calibration regression with G. For G, heldout predicted-vs-observed J is *transfer*; only `calibrate.py` evaluates same-prompt Taylor prediction.
9. Known Evil Pair `(L14,N8949,+),(L13,N7956,-)` deliberately omitted from all candidate generation and ranking. Can be externally tested only in a separately predeclared positive-control study. Finding these coordinates is **not** necessary for success.

## IV. Decision rule after pilot

Advance to larger layer coverage only when: (a) CUDA backward works reproducibly, (b) the selected method has a positive prompt-averaged heldout effect beyond own-sign layer-matched controls, (c) changes survive at least one blinded free-generation comparison **without disproportionate fluency/task losses**, and (d) observed same-prompt finite differences agree with Jacobian locally. Negative results are reported, not iteratively 'fixed' on the heldout split.

## V. Interpretation and validity

- Results do not establish unique 'semantic neuron locations', stable personality, causal mediation, global optimality or exact top-10 completeness.
- Tokenizer context segmentation affects phrase-level tests; q+ and q- are distributions on **token IDs** and are not phrase probabilities.
- Expression aggressiveness can correlate with incompetence or refusal. Always report invalid/degenerate outputs.
- No human training dataset is used for L/D. C uses human-written contrast examples and thus is *not* a strict data-free approach.
- AutoDL CUDA backward for Qwen3.5/FLA has not been verified in this artifact environment.
